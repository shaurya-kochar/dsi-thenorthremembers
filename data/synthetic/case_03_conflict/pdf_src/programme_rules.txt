Hyderabad Runners - Half Marathon Training Programme (HMTP), Batch 2026B: Eligibility and Schedule

Batch 2026B begins on 8 November 2026 and runs for 12 weeks, ending 31 January 2027. Sessions are held every Saturday and Sunday at 5:30 AM at KBR Park.

A participant is eligible for Batch 2026B only if ALL of the following conditions hold.

Rule 1 (Membership): the participant is an Active member of Hyderabad Runners whose membership is valid at least until 31 January 2027.

Rule 2 (Medical clearance): the participant holds a medical clearance dated within the 12 months before the batch start date.

Rule 3 (Registration): the registration for Batch 2026B was submitted on or before 15 September 2026.

Rule 4 (Running background): the participant completed a 10K event in the 12 months before the batch start date.
